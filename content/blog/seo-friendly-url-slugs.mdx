An SEO-friendly URL slug is the short, readable part of a web address that identifies a specific page, written in lowercase words separated by hyphens and containing the page's main keyword. For example, in `example.com/blog/running-shoe-guide`, the slug is `running-shoe-guide`. Good slugs are concise, descriptive and stable over time.

This guide explains what makes a slug search-friendly and how to create clean slugs in seconds with the free [URL Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator).

## What is a URL slug

The slug is the final segment of a URL path, after the domain and any folders. Content management systems usually create one automatically from the page title, but auto-generated slugs often include dates, IDs, punctuation remnants or filler words. Editing the slug before you publish is one of the easiest on-page SEO wins.

## Why slugs matter for SEO

URLs are a minor ranking factor on their own, but a good slug helps in several practical ways:

- **Clarity for users.** People see URLs in search results, shared links and browser bars. A readable slug signals what they'll get.
- **Context for search engines.** A descriptive slug reinforces the page topic alongside the title and headings.
- **Cleaner sharing.** Short URLs are easier to paste into emails, chats and social posts without looking suspicious.
- **Easier maintenance.** Logical slugs make your site structure easier to audit and internally link.

## URL slug best practices

### Use lowercase letters

Some servers treat uppercase and lowercase URLs as different pages, which can create duplicates. Lowercase avoids the problem entirely.

### Separate words with hyphens

Google recommends hyphens rather than underscores to separate words in URLs. Hyphens are read as word separators, while underscores may cause words to be read as one long term. So `keyword-research-tips` is better than `keyword_research_tips`.

### Keep it short and focused

Aim for a slug of roughly three to five meaningful words. There's no official limit, but shorter slugs are easier to read and less likely to be truncated in search results.

### Include the primary keyword

Use the main topic phrase, not the full headline. A post titled "10 Best Running Shoes for 2026: The Complete Guide" might use `best-running-shoes`.

### Think twice before adding dates

Years in slugs date your content and force a URL change when you update it. Keep dates in the title, where they're easy to edit.

### Remove special characters

Punctuation, emoji and accented characters can become encoded strings like `%C3%A9` that are hard to read. Stick to plain letters, numbers and hyphens.

## When to keep stop words

Removing stop words usually makes slugs tighter, but check that the result still reads naturally. "how-choose-right-trail-shoe" is understandable, while a title like "Out of Office Message Examples" loses meaning as "out-office-message-examples". If a stop word carries meaning, keep it.

## How to change a URL slug safely

Changing the slug of a published page creates a new URL. To avoid losing traffic and links:

- Set up a 301 redirect from the old URL to the new one.
- Update internal links so they point directly to the new URL.
- Make sure the canonical tag references the new URL. The [Canonical Tag Generator](/tools/canonical-generator) can help.
- Update your XML sitemap.

Only change slugs when there's a clear benefit, such as removing an outdated year or fixing an unreadable auto-generated string.

## FAQ

### Should the slug match the page title exactly?

No. The slug should capture the core topic in a few words, while the title can be longer and more persuasive. Matching the primary keyword is enough.

### Are numbers okay in URL slugs?

Yes, numbers are fine when they're meaningful, such as a model number. For list posts, consider leaving the count out so you can add items later without the URL becoming inaccurate.

### Do slugs need to be unique?

Within the same folder, yes. Two pages can't share the same full URL. Most content systems add a suffix like "-2" when a slug is taken, which is worth replacing with something descriptive.

### Does slug length affect rankings?

There's no evidence that a particular length is rewarded. Shorter slugs are simply easier for people to read, remember and share.
