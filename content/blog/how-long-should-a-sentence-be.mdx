There's no strict rule for how long a sentence should be, but most readable web content averages about 15 to 20 words per sentence. Sentences over 25 words are where readers start to struggle, so treat those as candidates for splitting. The aim is a comfortable average with a natural mix of short and longer sentences.

To see where your draft stands, paste it into the free [Long Sentence Detector](/tools/long-sentence-detector). It shows your average sentence length and lists every sentence that runs past the word limit you choose.

## Why sentence length matters

Readers hold a sentence in short-term memory until they reach the end and understand it. The longer the sentence, the more they have to juggle. Long sentences:

- **Bury the main point** under clauses and qualifications.
- **Increase the chance of misreading**, especially on mobile screens where a single sentence can fill the view.
- **Raise readability scores** like Flesch-Kincaid and Gunning Fog, which both weigh words per sentence.

If you're working on overall clarity, our [readability guide](/blog/readability-guide) covers sentence length alongside word choice and structure.

## Ideal sentence length by content type

These are general guidelines rather than hard limits:

| Content type | Typical average | Flag sentences over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing pages and ads | 10–15 words | 20 words |
| Blog posts and web content | 15–20 words | 25 words |
| Guides and reports | 18–22 words | 30 words |
| Academic writing | 20+ words | 35 words |

Notice that the goal is an average. A page made entirely of 15-word sentences would sound monotonous. Variety is part of good rhythm.

## How to shorten long sentences

### Split at conjunctions

Words like "and", "but", "because" and "which" often mark a natural break point.

### Cut filler phrases

Phrases like "in order to", "due to the fact that" and "at this point in time" add words without adding meaning. "To", "because" and "now" do the same job.

### Turn lists into lists

If a sentence contains four or five items separated by commas, it may work better as a bulleted list.

### Move asides into their own sentence

Parenthetical remarks and long "which" clauses can usually stand alone. Give them their own sentence, or cut them if they're not essential.

### Rewrite passive constructions

Passive voice adds helper verbs and often a "by" phrase. Switching to active voice shortens the sentence and makes it clearer. The [Passive Voice Checker](/tools/passive-voice-checker) can find these for you.

## Common mistakes

- **Making every sentence short.** A string of very short sentences feels choppy. Mix lengths.
- **Fixing length with semicolons.** Swapping "and" for a semicolon doesn't make a sentence any easier to read. Use a full stop.
- **Ignoring meaning.** Splitting a sentence can break the logical link between ideas. Add a transition such as "so" or "however" if you need to restore it.
- **Counting headings and lists.** Text without end punctuation can be read as one very long sentence. Check your body copy for the most accurate picture.

## What about run-on sentences?

A run-on sentence is a grammar problem, where two independent clauses are joined without proper punctuation. A long sentence is a readability problem. A sentence can be grammatically correct and still too long to read comfortably. The Long Sentence Detector looks only at length, so it catches both long-but-correct sentences and many run-ons that happen to be long.

## FAQ

### Is a 30-word sentence too long?

Not necessarily. A well-structured 30-word sentence can be clear. But if many of your sentences run that long, your average will climb and readers will notice. Use long sentences sparingly.

### What's the best threshold to use?

For general web content, start with the default of 25 words. Lower it to 20 for marketing copy, or raise it to 30 or more for academic and technical writing.

### Does sentence length affect SEO?

Search engines don't rank pages by sentence length. Shorter, clearer sentences do make content easier to read, though, and readable content tends to keep visitors engaged.
