On-page SEO is everything you can optimize directly on a page to help it rank and earn clicks. Unlike backlinks, it's entirely within your control — which makes it the highest-leverage place to start.

## Title tags

Your title tag is the single most important on-page element. Keep it under 60 characters, put your primary keyword near the front, and make it compelling. Check yours with the [SEO Title Analyzer](/tools/seo-title-analyzer).

## Meta descriptions

Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings, but they heavily influence click-through rate. Aim for 120–155 characters, include your keyword, and end with a call to action. Preview both in a real [SERP snippet](/tools/serp-preview).

## Heading structure

Use a single `H1` per page, then organize content with `H2` and `H3` headings. A logical hierarchy helps both readers and search engines understand your content. Audit yours with the [Heading Checker](/tools/heading-checker).

## Content quality and depth

Great on-page SEO starts with great content. Cover the topic thoroughly, answer related questions, and write at a reading level your audience is comfortable with — usually grade 6–8. The [Readability Score](/tools/readability-score) tool helps you hit that mark.

## Internal linking

Link between related pages using descriptive anchor text. Internal links spread authority around your site and help search engines discover content.

## URL slugs

Keep URLs short, lowercase and keyword-rich. Generate clean ones with the [Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator).

## Structured data

Adding [schema markup](/tools/schema-generator) unlocks rich results — star ratings, FAQs, prices — that make your listing stand out in search.

## The on-page checklist

- [ ] Unique, keyword-focused title under 60 characters
- [ ] Compelling meta description (120–155 chars)
- [ ] One H1, logical H2/H3 structure
- [ ] Primary keyword in the first 100 words
- [ ] Descriptive, short URL slug
- [ ] Internal links to related pages
- [ ] Alt text on images
- [ ] Structured data where relevant

Work through this list for every important page, and you'll have a solid on-page foundation.
