## What counts as an extra space

"Extra spaces" is a catch-all for any horizontal whitespace you didn't mean to type. The most common kinds are:

- **Double spaces between words**, often from copy-pasting or from editing a sentence and leaving a gap behind.
- **Two spaces after a period**, a habit many people learned on typewriters.
- **Leading spaces** at the start of a line, usually left over from indentation in another document.
- **Trailing spaces** at the end of a line. These are invisible, which is exactly why they cause problems.
- **Tabs mixed with spaces**, common when text comes from spreadsheets, PDFs or code editors.

## Why extra whitespace matters

A stray double space looks minor, but it adds up:

- **Professional polish.** Inconsistent spacing makes published copy, emails and documents look careless.
- **Data quality.** "London" and "London " look identical but are different values to a spreadsheet, database or deduplication script. Trailing spaces are a frequent cause of failed lookups and duplicate records.
- **Consistent character counts.** If you're writing to a limit, such as a meta description or a social post, wasted spaces eat into it. A tool like the [Character Counter](/tools/character-counter) will count every one of them.
- **Cleaner HTML and CMS input.** Browsers usually collapse repeated spaces when rendering, but the raw content stored in your CMS stays messy and can surface in feeds, exports and plain-text emails.

## Best practices for cleaning up spacing

### Clean before you deduplicate

If you're tidying a list of keywords, emails or product names, remove extra spaces *first*. Duplicate-line tools compare lines exactly, so "running shoes" and "running shoes " won't match until the trailing space is gone. A good order is: remove extra spaces, then [Remove Duplicate Lines](/tools/remove-duplicate-lines), then sort.

### Watch out for non-standard spaces

Some text, especially from web pages and word processors, contains non-breaking spaces or other special space characters. These look like regular spaces but aren't the plain space or tab character, so a basic whitespace cleaner may leave them in place. If spacing still looks off after cleaning, retype the affected gap or paste the text into a plain-text editor first.

### Keep one space after periods

Modern style guides, including most web and publishing styles, recommend a single space after a sentence-ending period. The tool enforces this automatically by collapsing double spaces.

### Review structured content

Trimming is great for prose and lists, but think twice with code, poetry or anything that relies on alignment. When in doubt, clean a small sample and compare.

## FAQ

### Will removing extra spaces affect my SEO?

Not directly. Search engines normalize whitespace when they parse a page, so double spaces aren't a ranking issue. The benefit is indirect: cleaner copy reads better, and accurate character counts help you keep titles and meta descriptions within commonly recommended lengths.

### Can I remove all spaces, not just extra ones?

This tool keeps one space between words so your text stays readable. If you need text with no spaces at all, for example to build a URL, try the [Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator), which converts text into hyphenated, URL-friendly slugs.

### How do I find trailing spaces I can't see?

Paste your text into the tool and check the "Characters removed" metric. If it's above zero even though the text looked fine, there were invisible leading, trailing or repeated spaces.

### Does it work on very long documents?

Yes. Processing happens locally in your browser, so you can paste long documents or large lists without uploading them anywhere.
