To count sentences, look for terminal punctuation: each period, question mark or exclamation point that ends a thought marks one sentence. A sentence counter automates this and also divides your word count by your sentence count to give an average sentence length, which is the more useful number for judging how readable your writing is.

## What a sentence count tells you

On its own, the number of sentences in a document says little. Two 500-word articles could have 20 sentences or 40. The difference between them is **average sentence length**, measured in words per sentence.

- **Lower averages** usually mean text is easier to scan and understand.
- **Higher averages** often signal dense, complex prose that asks more of the reader.

Readability formulas such as Flesch Reading Ease use average sentence length as one of their two main inputs, alongside word complexity. If you want to know why sentence length matters so much, our [readability guide](/blog/readability-guide) explains the theory.

## How sentences are counted

Automated tools split text at sentence-ending punctuation. The RankBoost tool treats a run of text that ends in a period, exclamation mark or question mark, followed by a space or the end of the text, as one sentence. Text after the final punctuation mark counts as a sentence too, so an unfinished last line is still included.

This approach is accurate for normal prose, but a few patterns can throw any counter off:

- **Abbreviations** such as "Dr." or "e.g." may be read as sentence endings.
- **Lists and headings** without punctuation can merge with the next sentence.
- **Ellipses and repeated punctuation** are treated as a single ending, which is usually what you want.

For the cleanest result, paste body text without headings or bullet lists.

## What is a good average sentence length

There is no single correct figure, but for web content a common guideline is an average of around 15 to 20 words per sentence. Here is a rough way to interpret your result:

| Average words per sentence | Typical feel |
| --- | --- |
| Under 12 | Very punchy; can feel choppy if overused |
| 12 to 20 | Comfortable for most online readers |
| 21 to 25 | Getting dense; fine for expert audiences |
| Over 25 | Likely hard to follow for general readers |

Audience matters. Technical documentation or academic writing can run longer, while marketing copy and mobile content usually benefit from shorter sentences.

## Best practices for sentence length

### Vary your rhythm

An average is only half the story. Ten sentences of exactly 15 words each read like a metronome. Mix short sentences for emphasis with longer ones that develop an idea. The bar chart makes monotony easy to spot: if every bar is the same height, add variety.

### Split your longest sentences

If your longest sentence is well above your average, it is a good candidate to break up. Look for natural break points at "and", "but", "which" and semicolons. For a full list of overly long sentences, run your text through the [Long Sentence Detector](/tools/long-sentence-detector).

### Keep one idea per sentence

Long sentences often try to carry two or three ideas. Give each idea its own sentence and your reader will process them faster.

## Common mistakes

- **Chasing a perfect average.** A 16-word average with a few 45-word sentences can still be hard to read. Fix the outliers.
- **Over-correcting into fragments.** Very short sentences everywhere can sound abrupt and childish.
- **Ignoring paragraph structure.** Sentence length and paragraph length work together. Check paragraphs with the [Paragraph Counter](/tools/paragraph-counter) as well.

## FAQ

### Does a question count as a sentence?

Yes. Any thought ending in a question mark is counted as a sentence, just like one ending in a period or exclamation point.

### How do I count sentences in a bulleted list?

List items without end punctuation may be merged together by an automated counter. If list items matter for your count, add periods to them or count them separately.

### Is a longer average sentence length bad for SEO?

Search engines do not use sentence length as a direct ranking factor. However, clear, readable text tends to keep visitors engaged, which supports the goal of answering their question well.

### What is the difference between a sentence and a line?

A line ends at a line break, while a sentence ends at terminal punctuation. One line can hold several sentences, and one sentence can wrap across several lines.
